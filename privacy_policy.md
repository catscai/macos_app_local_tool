# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 10, 2026

SonicLab ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the SonicLab app (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

1.1 Information You Provide
- Custom effects, effect names, descriptions, and other content you create in the App.
- Preferences and settings, such as theme, language, and app options.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- Basic app usage data, such as feature interactions, app version, device type, operating system version, and crash information.
- Advertising-related information if ads are shown in your region and you are using the free version of the App.
- Cloud sync metadata, such as timestamps and record identifiers, when iCloud sync is enabled.

1.3 Local Media and Files
- The App may access audio files, folders, or media library items that you explicitly choose or authorize.
- Your local audio files are primarily processed on-device.
- We do not upload your local audio files to our servers unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:
- Provide and operate the App.
- Save and sync your custom effects, favorites, recent effects, and preferences.
- Improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.
- Support Pro features and restore purchases.
- Show ads in the free version where applicable.
- Comply with legal obligations.

3. iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, certain app data may be stored in your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit services. This may include:
- Custom effects
- Favorite effects
- Recent effects
- Preferences and settings

Your iCloud data is handled through Apple's infrastructure and is associated with your Apple ID. We do not have direct access to your private iCloud account beyond the data necessary to provide synchronization functionality.

4. Advertising

The free version of the App may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

These services may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and other information as permitted by applicable law and their own privacy policies in order to provide and measure ads.

Pro users are not shown ads.

5. Purchases

If you purchase SonicLab Pro, payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

We may receive limited information from Apple, such as purchase status or subscription status, for the purpose of unlocking paid features and restoring purchases.

6. Data Storage

Some data is stored locally on your device, including:
    - App settings
    - Custom effects
    - Favorites
    - Recent activity
    - Export-related preferences

If iCloud sync is enabled, certain supported data may also be stored in your iCloud account.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:
    - With Apple, for iCloud sync and App Store purchases
    - With advertising providers, if ads are enabled
    - With service providers that help us operate, analyze, or improve the App
    - If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety

8. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to provide the App and its features.
Data stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, remove the App, or reset relevant settings.
Data synced through iCloud may remain in your iCloud account until you delete it or disable sync, subject to Apple's policies.

9. Your Choices

You can:
    - Disable iCloud sync
    - Delete custom effects and local app data
    - Reset app settings
    - Upgrade to Pro to remove ads
    - Manage permissions for media access in your device settings

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International Users

Depending on your region, the App may use third-party services that process data in countries outside your own. By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred and processed in accordance with applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the effective date and provide notice where required.
