# Privacy Policy

DroidBridge respects your privacy.

## Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Local Data Usage

The app may access local files (such as the Downloads folder) only for the purpose of transferring files between your Apple device and your Android device.

For example, files such as photos, videos, and documents selected by the user can be transferred between devices.

All data processing happens locally on your device. No data is transmitted to any external server.

## Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

## Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us.
