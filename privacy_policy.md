# Privacy Policy for CC Browser

Last updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CC Browser ("CC Browser", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the CC Browser iOS app.

CC Browser is designed as a local-first browser. Most browsing data is stored on your device. We do not operate a separate CC Browser account system, and we do not run our own server for collecting your browsing history, bookmarks, AI conversation index, downloads, or site identity data. Some features, however, depend on Apple services, Google advertising services, the websites you visit, search providers, AI platforms you choose to open, and other third-party services. Those services may process data under their own privacy policies.

## Information Stored by the App

CC Browser may store the following information locally on your device:

- Browsing history, bookmarks, recent searches, recent tabs, home page shortcuts, and browser preferences.
- Download records, downloaded files, extracted archives, resource scan results, local video playback state, and files you choose to open or preview.
- Private Space data, including private browsing history, bookmarks, searches, tabs, downloads, and settings.
- Site Profiles data, including website names, website hosts, profile names, profile colors, and identifiers used to keep separate website login containers.
- AI Workbench settings, allowed AI platforms, recent AI conversations, AI conversation search index, AI conversation favorites, and exported AI conversation files.
- Monetization status, including Pro entitlement cache, Download Pack entitlement cache, ad-free pass expiry, rewarded ad counters, and VR usage counters.
- App settings such as theme, language, search engine preference, download preferences, content blocking settings, and iCloud sync preferences.

Some of this information may include user content or sensitive browsing information depending on what you choose to save, download, visit, index, or enter into the app.

## Browsing and Website Data

CC Browser lets you browse the open web. When you visit a website, that website may receive information normally sent by a browser, such as your IP address, URL requests, cookies, login state, browser headers, and interactions with that website. This processing is controlled by the website, not by CC Browser.

WebView storage, including cookies and local website data, is kept by iOS WebKit on your device. Site Profiles may use separate WebKit data stores so that different profiles for the same website can keep separate login states. CC Browser stores profile metadata locally, but website cookies and login tokens belong to the relevant website and WebKit container.

Incognito mode is intended to reduce local saving of browsing activity in CC Browser. Websites, network providers, search engines, and other third parties may still be able to observe activity as part of normal web access.

## AI Features

CC Browser includes AI Workbench and "Ask AI" features. When you open an AI platform, interact with it, or send content to it, that platform may receive the information you provide, the page link or page text you choose to send, and normal browser request information.

If you enable AI conversation history indexing, CC Browser may collect visible conversation content from supported AI platforms that you have allowed. The indexed content is stored locally so that you can search, continue, favorite, and export conversations. AI conversation indexing is opt-in and can be limited to selected platforms. Incognito mode does not collect AI conversation history. Normal Space and Private Space use separate AI conversation storage.

CC Browser does not send your AI conversation index to our own server. If you use iCloud sync for relevant app data in the future, Apple may process synced data through iCloud under Apple's policies.

## Downloads, Resource Scan, HTML Preview, OCR, and Media Tools

When you use downloads or resource scanning, CC Browser may request webpages, media files, scripts, images, or other resources from the target website in order to show scan results or download files. The target website may receive normal web request information.

HTML Preview reads the HTML content you type or select from a local file and renders it inside the app. Local files you choose remain on your device unless you share or upload them.

Local video and VR video tools process the media files or URLs you choose. Free users may have limited VR uses per day; rewarded ads may grant additional VR uses. These counters are stored locally.

If you use QR scanning, OCR, camera-based scanning, image saving, or media import features, the app may request access to your camera, photos, or selected files. We use that access only to provide the feature you requested. Content selected from your device is not uploaded to our own server by CC Browser.

## iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, selected app data may be synced through Apple iCloud/CloudKit, including bookmarks, browsing history, recent searches, recent tabs, home page shortcuts, hidden built-in sites, and search engine preference. Private Space sync is optional and only syncs when you enable it.

iCloud data is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policies and your iCloud account settings. CC Browser does not operate the iCloud servers.

## Private Space

Private Space is designed to separate private browsing data from Normal Space. Private Space data may be encrypted locally using keys stored in the iOS Keychain and may use iOS file protection. If you enable Private Space iCloud sync, the selected Private Space data may also be synced through iCloud.

Private Space is a local privacy feature. It does not make you anonymous to websites, network providers, search engines, AI platforms, advertisers, or other third parties you interact with.

## Advertising

CC Browser uses Google AdMob to display ads, including banner ads, native ads, app open ads, and rewarded ads. Ads may appear on the home page, Workbench, AI Workbench, history list, app launch, or in rewarded ad flows such as ad-free passes and VR-use rewards.

Google and its partners may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers where permitted, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions with ads, diagnostics, and other information used to deliver, measure, limit, and improve ads. Advertising data practices are controlled by Google and its partners.

If your device or region provides advertising consent or tracking choices, your choices may affect whether personalized ads or identifiers such as IDFA are used. Even when personalized ads or tracking are limited, non-personalized or contextual ads may still be shown unless you have Pro access or an active ad-free pass.

Pro users and users with an active rewarded ad-free pass do not see in-app ads during the active entitlement period, subject to the app's current entitlement state.

## Purchases and Subscriptions

CC Browser may offer in-app purchases and subscriptions, including CC Browser Pro and feature packs. Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your full payment card number or Apple ID password.

The app stores entitlement status locally, such as whether Pro or a feature pack is active, so that it can unlock paid features. Apple may provide transaction status and subscription information needed to validate and restore purchases.

## Search and Suggestions

When you search from CC Browser, your query is sent to the search engine or suggestion provider you choose. Those providers may process your query, IP address, device/browser request information, and related data under their own privacy policies.

Recent searches may be stored locally, and may be synced through iCloud if iCloud sync is enabled.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

CC Browser may share or make data available only in the following situations:

- With third-party services you choose to use, such as websites, search engines, AI platforms, and file or media services.
- With Apple services, such as iCloud/CloudKit, StoreKit, App Store purchase processing, and system permissions.
- With Google AdMob and advertising partners for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and rewarded ads.
- When you manually export, share, copy, download, or upload content.
- If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

## Retention and Deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, clear it in the app, remove related records, reset the relevant feature, or uninstall the app. Downloaded files may remain in the folder you selected until you delete them.

iCloud-synced data may remain in iCloud according to your iCloud settings and Apple's retention practices. You can disable iCloud sync or delete synced data through app settings, iCloud settings, or by deleting the related local records where supported.

Advertising, purchase, website, search engine, and AI platform data are retained by the relevant third parties according to their own privacy policies.

## Security

CC Browser uses local storage, iOS WebKit data stores, iOS Keychain, encryption for certain Private Space data, and system file protection where applicable. These measures are intended to protect data stored by the app, but no app, device, network, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting your device passcode, Apple ID, iCloud account, and accounts you log into on websites or AI platforms.

## Children's Privacy

CC Browser is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to use online services without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own server. Websites, ads, search engines, and third-party services used through the browser may have their own age requirements and privacy practices.

## International Data Transfers

Because CC Browser lets you access websites, AI platforms, search engines, advertising services, Apple services, and other third-party services, your data may be processed in countries or regions other than where you live. Those transfers are controlled by the relevant third-party service providers and their privacy policies.

## Your Choices

You can control many privacy-related features in the app and iOS settings:

- Clear browsing history, recent searches, downloads, or related records where supported.
- Use Incognito mode for sessions you do not want CC Browser to save locally.
- Enable or disable AI conversation history indexing, and choose which AI platforms are indexed.
- Delete AI conversation records, favorites, or exported files where supported.
- Use Private Space to separate private app data from Normal Space.
- Enable or disable Private Space iCloud sync.
- Manage Site Profiles and delete website profile metadata.
- Manage iCloud sync in app settings and iOS settings.
- Manage app permissions such as camera, photos, local network, and tracking in iOS Settings.
- Purchase Pro or use an active ad-free pass to remove in-app ads.
