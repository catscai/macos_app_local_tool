# Bible & Wonder Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 20, 2026

Bible & Wonder is operated by Cai Yanqing (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the Bible & Wonder iOS app handles information when you read Scripture, save passages and notes, use Peace, play games, or view ads.

## 1. Information stored on your device

Bible & Wonder can be used without creating an account. The app stores your reading position and activity, bookmarks, highlights, notes and tags, quiz and game progress, scores, and preferences on your device. The app uses this information to restore your progress and show your My Journey summary.

The app uses your selections in Peace to show relevant Scripture. We do not send your Peace selections, personal notes, or saved Scripture collection to Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob.

If you choose to share a passage or your My Journey summary, the app passes the content you select to the iOS share sheet. Any service or recipient you choose may handle that content under its own privacy practices.

## 2. Usage analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people use Bible & Wonder and to improve the app. Analytics is enabled by default. It collects events such as app launches, sessions, and which main tabs are opened. It may also collect app version, device and operating system information, approximate region, and app instance or device identifiers. We use analytics to understand feature usage and identify areas for improvement.

## 3. Crash reporting

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix crashes. Crash reporting is enabled by default. Crash reports may include stack traces, app and operating system versions, device information, installation identifiers, and recent app events that help diagnose a crash.

## 4. Advertising and advertising choices

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads and to offer optional rewarded ads in certain games. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location, device or app identifiers, technical information, and information about ads you see or interact with. This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad appears, and detect fraud. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices, applicable requirements, and the information available to Google.

Where Google requires an advertising privacy choice, the app provides a consent message and an **Ad privacy options** entry at the bottom of the **Play** tab. You can use that entry, when available, to review or change your advertising choices.

## 5. Service providers and external links

Google receives information through the analytics, crash reporting, and advertising services described above. We require service providers that receive personal information through the app to protect it in accordance with applicable law and to provide protection consistent with this policy. Google provides more details about its services here:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

When you open an external link, the destination service handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

## 6. Retention, deletion, and privacy requests

Your reading and game records remain on your device until you remove them through an available app feature or delete the app. A copy may remain in a device backup according to your backup settings.

Information processed through Google services is retained according to the relevant service settings and Google’s retention policies. For example, Firebase Crashlytics retains crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from its live and backup systems.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a request, email catscaicai@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify or locate the data related to your request. Some analytics or diagnostic records cannot be matched to an individual.

We will publish updates at this URL and revise the effective date when this policy changes.
