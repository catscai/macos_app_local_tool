1. Information We Collect and Process

1.1 Information You Create and Store in the App
When you use the App, you may create, edit, or save content such as:

- History records, result snapshots, and gameplay configurations

This information is primarily stored locally on your device to provide the App’s core features.

1.2 iCloud Sync Data
If you choose to enable iCloud sync, the App may use Apple CloudKit to synchronize certain data with your private iCloud database associated with your Apple ID. Synced data may include:
- Custom templates
- Certain history records

We do not sync this data unless you choose to enable iCloud sync.

As part of ad delivery and related services, third-party advertising providers may collect or process certain information under their own privacy policies, such as:

- IP address
- Device and operating system information

- Technical information used for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency capping, and measurement

Such information is handled by the relevant third-party provider in accordance with its own privacy practices.

1.4 In-App Purchases
If you purchase Pro membership or restore purchases, the App uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to process transactions and verify purchase status. We may access information such as:
- Whether a purchase has been made
- Which premium entitlements are active on your device/account

We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details or billing account credentials.

1.5 Shared Content
If you choose to use the sharing feature, the App may pass selected content, such as result text, template summaries, or generated share images, to the iOS/iPadOS system share sheet and then to the third-party app or service you choose.
